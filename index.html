AC Repair in Wichita Falls

Furnace Repair in Wichita Falls

When the furnace clicks but never lights, lights and shuts off, or runs without warming the house, the cause is usually one component. We test the ignition, airflow, and safety controls to find it. Every visit includes a safety check of the burners and venting, and you'll know the price before we start.

  • No ignition when the heat calls
  • Short cycling on and off

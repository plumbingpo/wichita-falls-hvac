Upfront Pricing

Straight Answers and Upfront Prices

Effective HVAC service starts with knowing exactly what you're dealing with. Wichita Falls HVAC provides honest, upfront pricing across Wichita Falls, handling everything from a worn capacitor to a full system replacement. Every job begins with a careful inspection of your equipment, including its age, tonnage, fuel type, refrigerant charge, and duct condition, followed by a clear recommendation and a written price before any work starts.

Most HVAC failures send small signals well before the system stops. Keep an eye out for rising utility bills, weak airflow at the registers, frost on the copper lines, or a bedroom that never catches up to the thermostat. Behind those symptoms are usually slow refrigerant leaks, worn blower bearings, or a capacitor on its way out. A quick inspection at the first sign often costs far less than a failed compressor.

We also help homeowners plan ahead. Most air conditioners and furnaces last 12 to 18 years, and a planned replacement is nearly always cheaper and less stressful than an emergency swap during a heat wave or cold snap. Our technicians know the climate demands common to Wichita Falls and how they affect equipment life, so we can tell you honestly whether your system has years left or is living on borrowed time.

Maintenance Plans

Tune-Ups That Keep Your System Running

Routine maintenance is the most reliable way to keep your home comfortable year-round. Regular service heads off failures before they start, keeps coils and burners clean, and catches small faults while they're still cheap to fix. At Wichita Falls HVAC, every plan is built around your home's specific equipment, with scheduled visits that cover coil cleaning, refrigerant charge verification, capacitor and contactor testing, burner and heat exchanger inspection, condensate drain clearing, filter replacement, and a static pressure check across the duct system.

Every maintenance plan we write is a little different. An older furnace needs a closer look than a new heat pump, and a dusty, high-pollen area means condenser coils foul faster. We build spring cooling and fall heating visits around your system, your climate, and how your family uses it, so every tune-up keeps the system efficient and extends the life of the equipment you've already paid for.

The savings add up faster than most people expect. Clean coils move heat more efficiently, a correct refrigerant charge takes strain off the compressor, and a fresh filter protects the blower motor. Compared with the cost of replacing a system even one year early, a maintenance plan is a bargain.

Proper Sizing

Comfort Sized to Your Home's Demand

An undersized system runs nonstop and never catches up on the hottest days, while an oversized one short-cycles, wastes energy, and leaves the house humid. Wichita Falls HVAC sizes every replacement and new installation to your actual home. That means a proper load calculation based on square footage, insulation, window exposure, and duct layout, rather than simply matching the tonnage of the old unit. For ductless mini-splits, it means matching head capacity to each zone so every room holds its setpoint.

Homes change over time. An addition or a finished basement can outgrow the single-stage system that used to be plenty. Modern two-stage and variable-speed heat pumps hold tighter temperatures and use far less energy. If you're torn between a heat pump and a furnace-and-AC pair, or ducted and ductless, we'll explain the real trade-offs in SEER2 ratings, operating cost, electrical and venting needs, and installation work.

Frequent Failures

Common HVAC Problems We Fix in Wichita Falls

Warm air from the vents, a furnace that refuses to light, a frozen evaporator coil, leaking refrigerant: each of these problems has a different cause and calls for its own repair. Wichita Falls HVAC diagnoses the exact fault before replacing anything, whether it's a condenser that hums but won't start, a furnace that short-cycles, a thermostat that keeps losing its program, a clogged condensate drain, or a mini-split flashing error codes.

Some failures can wait a day, and some can't. A bad capacitor only keeps the compressor from starting. A cracked heat exchanger, on the other hand, can let combustion gases into the house, and a blocked condensate drain can soak through a ceiling before anyone notices. Our technicians carry the parts to swap capacitors, contactors, fan motors, igniters, flame sensors, and control boards, seal ductwork that's leaking air into the attic, and recharge refrigerant once a leak has been repaired.
